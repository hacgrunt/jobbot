# Crypto PMM Metrics, Measurement & Operations

## The Crypto PMM Metrics Stack

### Vanity Metrics vs. Real Metrics

**Vanity (track but don't optimize for):**
* Discord member count
* Twitter follower count
* Total wallet addresses
* TVL in isolation (without retention context)
* "Partnership" announcements without integration depth

**Real Metrics (optimize for):**
* Daily/weekly active addresses (returning users, not one-time)
* Protocol revenue and fee generation
* Developer activity (new repos, commits, active developers)
* Governance participation rate
* Retention rate (users who return after 7/30/90 days)
* Integration depth (how many protocols compose with yours)
* Documentation usage and completion rates
* Support ticket trends (decreasing = better UX/docs)
* Community quality (engagement rate, not size)
* Airdrop-to-retained-user conversion rate

### Metrics by Protocol Type

**DeFi Protocols:**
* TVL (with context — is it mercenary or sticky?)
* Daily trading volume
* Unique traders/LPs per day
* Protocol revenue (fees collected)
* Capital efficiency ratios
* LP retention rate
* Integration count and TVL from integrated protocols

**L1/L2 Chains:**
* Daily active addresses
* Transaction count and gas fees
* Developer count (active GitHub contributors to ecosystem)
* Number of deployed contracts
* Cross-chain bridge volume (inflows vs. outflows)
* dApp diversity (not just DeFi — social, gaming, NFTs)
* Time to first transaction for new developers

**Infrastructure:**
* API call volume and reliability (uptime)
* Number of protocols/apps using the service
* Developer satisfaction scores
* Documentation completion and accuracy
* Migration rate from competitors
* Response time / performance benchmarks

**Consumer Apps:**
* DAU/MAU ratio
* Retention curves (D1, D7, D30, D90)
* Feature adoption rates
* Referral/organic growth rate
* App store ratings and review sentiment
* Revenue per user (if applicable)

## Crypto PMM Operating Cadence

### Daily
* Monitor CT narrative and sentiment
* Track competitor announcements and market moves
* Engage with community questions and discussions
* Review on-chain metrics dashboards
* Respond to governance discussions if relevant

### Weekly
* Content publishing (threads, blog posts, technical updates)
* Community call or AMA (if applicable)
* Internal metrics review
* Competitive intelligence brief
* Social media performance review

### Monthly
* Ecosystem report (for internal and/or public consumption)
* Developer metrics review (new developers, active projects, grant program status)
* Partnership pipeline review
* Content performance analysis (what resonated, what didn't)
* Community health assessment

### Quarterly
* Strategic positioning review (has the competitive landscape shifted?)
* Messaging refresh if needed
* Major content pieces (research reports, state-of-the-ecosystem)
* Conference and event planning
* Budget and resource allocation review

### Annually
* Full positioning and messaging overhaul assessment
* Brand perception study (qualitative — interviews, surveys)
* Annual ecosystem report
* GTM strategy reset for next year's roadmap

## The Crypto PMM Skill Tree

### Core Skills (Must Have)
1. **Crypto fluency:** Understand DeFi, L1/L2, tokenomics, governance at a technical level
2. **Narrative crafting:** Write compelling positioning that resonates with crypto-native audiences
3. **Community intuition:** Read the room on CT, Discord, governance forums
4. **Data literacy:** Build and interpret Dune dashboards, on-chain metrics
5. **Developer empathy:** Understand developer needs and evaluate documentation quality
6. **Speed:** Crypto moves fast. Marketing cycles are days/weeks, not quarters.

### Advanced Skills (Differentiators)
1. **Tokenomics literacy:** Understand how token design affects GTM strategy
2. **Governance strategy:** Frame governance proposals as product marketing
3. **Cross-ecosystem fluency:** Navigate multiple ecosystem cultures
4. **Regulatory awareness:** Know what you can and can't say about tokens
5. **Crisis communications:** Handle exploits, governance conflicts, market downturns
6. **Institutional translation:** Bridge degen culture and enterprise requirements

### AI-Era Skills (Emerging)
1. **AI agent marketing:** Positioning AI x crypto products, understanding agent frameworks
2. **AI-powered analytics:** Using AI tools for competitive intelligence and market analysis
3. **Automated content generation:** Leveraging AI for content production at crypto speed
4. **On-chain AI integration:** Understanding how AI models interact with blockchain protocols

## PMM Interview & Hiring Patterns in Crypto

### What Crypto Companies Look For

**Non-negotiable:**
* Genuine crypto knowledge (not surface-level). Can you talk about protocols, not just "blockchain technology"?
* CT presence or at least deep fluency with crypto social dynamics
* Portfolio of crypto-specific work (GTM plans, positioning docs, campaign examples)
* Ability to write for crypto audiences (not B2B SaaS style)

**Differentiators:**
* On-chain activity (your wallet IS your resume in crypto)
* Contributions to governance, open source, or community discussions
* Understanding of specific ecosystems relevant to the company
* Technical depth (can you read a smart contract? Do you understand gas mechanics?)
* Track record of shipping in fast-moving, ambiguous environments

### Common PMM Take-Home Assignments

**GTM Strategy:**
* "Design a GTM plan for our upcoming protocol upgrade/launch"
* Should include: positioning, target segments, channel strategy, timeline, success metrics, competitive positioning

**Positioning Exercise:**
* "How would you reposition our protocol given the current competitive landscape?"
* Should include: competitive analysis, positioning canvas, messaging hierarchy, proof points

**Campaign Design:**
* "Design a campaign to drive [specific metric] for our protocol"
* Should include: objective, target audience, channel mix, content plan, budget allocation, measurement framework

**Content Sample:**
* "Write a launch announcement thread for [feature]"
* "Create a migration guide for users moving from [competitor]"
* "Draft a governance proposal communication plan"

## PMM Org Structure in Crypto

### Early Stage (Pre-Series A, <50 people)
* Often no dedicated PMM — founder or head of marketing does PMM
* PMM work includes: positioning, launch messaging, content strategy, community strategy
* Generalist needed — someone who can write, analyze data, and talk to developers

### Growth Stage (Series A-B, 50-200 people)
* 1-3 dedicated PMMs
* Specialization begins: developer marketing vs. ecosystem marketing vs. consumer marketing
* PMM starts owning: competitive intelligence, launch playbooks, messaging frameworks
* Close collaboration with DevRel, BD, and community teams

### Mature Stage (Series C+, 200+ people)
* PMM team of 5-15
* Specialized roles: developer PMM, ecosystem PMM, institutional PMM, content/narrative lead
* PMM owns: positioning, competitive intel, launch process, messaging governance, analyst relations
* Regional PMM roles for key markets (Asia, Europe, LatAm)

## Crypto PMM vs. Traditional PMM

| Dimension | Traditional PMM | Crypto PMM |
|-----------|----------------|------------|
| Launch cycle | Quarterly | Weekly/monthly |
| Target audience | Buyers/users | Developers, traders, LPs, governance participants, researchers |
| Key channels | Sales enablement, website, campaigns | CT, Discord, governance forums, docs |
| Success metrics | Revenue, pipeline, win rate | TVL, active addresses, developer count, protocol revenue |
| Content style | Polished, brand-controlled | Authentic, transparent, community-native |
| Competitive intel | Quarterly reports | Real-time monitoring (on-chain + social) |
| Pricing | Feature-based | Tokenomics-driven, fee structure |
| Community role | Nice-to-have | Core to everything |
| Speed required | Moderate | Extreme |
| Regulatory overlay | Standard | Complex, evolving, jurisdiction-dependent |
