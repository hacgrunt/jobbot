## 2026-02-11 — Tempo Analysis: Marketing Deconstruction Pattern

**Context:** Pre-screener prep for Tempo (Stripe's stablecoin payments L1).

**Key meta-learning:** When analyzing a crypto project's positioning, the most useful framework is separating the *business motivation* from the *marketing narrative* from the *genuinely novel contribution*. For Tempo:
- Business motivation = vertical integration (Stripe owning the settlement layer before commoditization)
- Marketing narrative = "purpose-built payments chain the world needs"
- Genuinely novel = enterprise SLAs, compliance-at-protocol-level, distribution moat via Stripe's merchant network

**Pattern worth reusing:** For any fintech-to-crypto play (Circle Arc, Robinhood chain, Coinbase Base), ask: "Is this solving a problem or capturing a fee layer?" Usually both — and the best PMM positioning acknowledges the value creation while being honest about the value capture. Candidates who can articulate both sides without cynicism or naivety stand out.

**Framework that worked:** Claim-by-claim teardown (marketing spin vs. reality vs. what's actually useful) is a strong structure for competitive intel and interview prep. Forces specificity over vibes.
